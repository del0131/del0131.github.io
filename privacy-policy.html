Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Who we are

Atiidot (아띠닷) is a hand-poke beauty mark tattoo studio located in Gangnam, Seoul, Republic of Korea, operated by its owner. This policy covers the "Atiidot" developer application registered with the Pinterest API (App ID 1622888 and any successor application for the same tool). Contact: novelgold@gmail.com.

2. What the application is

The Atiidot application is a private, internal, single-user tool. It is used only by the owner of Atiidot, on her own Pinterest account (@atii_artmake_). It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up or login for any other person, and is never used to access any other Pinterest user's account or data.

3. What the application does

Using the Pinterest API (v5) with the owner's own OAuth authorization, the application:

  • reads the owner's own boards and pins;
  • creates pins on the owner's own boards, so content the owner already published on her own Instagram account can also be posted to her own Pinterest account;
  • updates titles, descriptions, and links of the owner's own pins.

The requested scopes are limited to this purpose: boards:read, pins:read, and pins:write.

4. Data we access and how we handle it

  • Only the owner's own data. The application accesses boards and pins belonging to the owner's Pinterest account only. It does not collect, request, or process personal data of any other person.
  • Tokens. OAuth access and refresh tokens for the owner's account are stored in secure credential storage and are used solely to perform the actions described above.
  • Content. Pin images, titles, and descriptions are the owner's own published content. They are processed transiently to create or update pins and are not retained beyond what the owner keeps in her own content library.
  • No sale or sharing. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any data with third parties, and no data is used for advertising profiling.
  • No tracking. The application sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking of any individual.

5. Retention and deletion

Access ends as soon as the owner revokes the application's authorization in Pinterest settings or deletes the stored tokens. Because the application processes only the owner's own account data, a deletion request is satisfied by revoking access and deleting stored credentials. Requests or questions can be sent to novelgold@gmail.com and will be answered within 30 days.

6. Security

Credentials are kept in encrypted, access-controlled storage. The application communicates with the Pinterest API over HTTPS only.

7. Children

The application is an internal business tool and is not directed at children or at any end user.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
